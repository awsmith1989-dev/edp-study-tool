Get told what a grader would say.

Work through all five sections of IEDC's ELED Project Plan template with AI feedback on each one — calibrated against the plan IEDC circulates as its own exemplar. Drafts save automatically. Export a clean PDF when it's ready to submit.

Section by section

Review each of the five sections as you write it, or run a full-plan review that weights coherence — whether your action steps recur in funding and timeline, and whether your metrics actually measure your objective.

Graded against what works

Feedback checks the things strong plans do: bounded objectives, named partners and data sources, honest acknowledgment of gaps, and policy advocacy that most candidates omit entirely.

Submission ready

Autosaves to your account so you can iterate over weeks. Export as a formatted PDF that matches the IEDC template when you're ready to attach it to your application.
